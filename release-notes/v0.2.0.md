## Highlights

- **Updates inside the app.** BreathPause now checks for new versions and installs them for you from Settings › About. A small pink dot on the bubble tells you when one is ready. This works from 0.2.0 on, so coming from 0.1.0 you need to install this one by hand.
- **Breathing music.** Seven soft sounds that follow your breathing pattern, from handpan to gentle waves. Turn them on with the speaker button on the bubble.
- **Edit the bubble right on your screen.** Hover over the bubble and click the pencil. Change its look, size, color and opacity, and drag the text where you want it.
- **A new settings window** with a live preview. Changes show up in the bubble right away, and Cancel takes them back.

## Also new

- The bear and the jellyfish open and close their eyes as they breathe
- Timer presets: Classic 25/5, Deep focus 50/10 and Short 15/3
- A separate font, size and color for each line of text
- Fresh colors: pink for breathing in, blue for breathing out, the same everywhere
- Hide the bubble with its own button; it keeps running in the tray

## Fixes

- The timer now pauses while your Windows PC is locked
- A text color set in Settings is no longer overridden by the bubble
